The plan in one paragraph

Lead with parents in Karnataka, because they create most profiles and respond to trust and privacy. Run three more audiences beside them: young professionals choosing for themselves, Kannada and South Indian NRIs, and marriage brokers for the partner program. Sell three things in every ad: ₹351 with no sales calls, your number stays private until both families agree, and your year only starts in March 2027. That last point matters because the app is young and has few profiles. It turns the honest "we're growing" message into a reason to join early.

Fix these before spending

  1. Pixel events. Done in code, not deployed yet. The site now sends Lead when a new email passes step 1 of registration, CompleteRegistration with the amount actually paid (₹351 or ₹301) after a paid sign-up, Purchase for credit packs and astrology, and SubmitApplication for partner sign-ups. After you deploy, check them in Events Manager → Test events.
  2. Ad link that opens registration. Done in code, not deployed yet. Use https://www.seetharamakalyana.in/?join=1 in every sign-up ad. It opens the register form instead of login. Add UTMs after it, e.g. /?join=1&utm_source=meta&utm_campaign=parents_kn.
  3. Set up the business side. Facebook Page and Instagram profile with the round logo, a Meta Business account, domain verification for seetharamakalyana.in, and WhatsApp Business on +91 8105710930 so "Send WhatsApp message" ads work.
  4. Fill the Page before ads run. Post 6–9 items from the calendar below first. People check the profile before paying ₹351, and an empty Page loses them.
Meta policy, read once. (1) Meta removed targeting by religion and caste in 2022, so you can't target "Hindu" or "Brahmin". Target by place, age and language, and let the creative carry the community message. (2) Ad text must not claim to know the viewer's religion, caste or relationship status. "Are you a single Brahmin?" gets rejected; "Profiles from 40+ communities" is fine. (3) Meta may treat matrimony as a dating service. If ads are rejected under the dating policy, apply for dating-ad permission through Meta's form and target 18+ only.

Reel scripts

Shoot on a phone, vertical, 15–30 seconds. Put text on screen because most people watch muted. End every reel on ad 11 for 2 seconds.

  2. She sighs, puts the phone down.
  3. Daughter shows her Seetha Rama Kalyana. Text: "Verified families. Number private until both agree."

After month one

  • Keep the two or three best ads running and replace the rest every 2–3 weeks.
  • Collect the first real success stories (with written consent) and turn them into reels. They will beat every graphic.
  • Add Telugu and Tamil versions of ads 03 and 06 if Bengaluru shows demand from those families.
  • Before March 2027, retire ad 05 and replace the message with your next offer.
  • Launch a Sankranti campaign in early January, when many families restart the search for the next wedding season.
